## [0.1.0]

### Added

- Emoji-themed pipeline output with 6 built-in themes (default, space, ocean, lab, food, pirate)
- Live progress bar with themed emojis
- Seasonal greetings with festive day countdown
- Pipeline completion summary with per-process stats
- Channel operators: `emojiView` and `emojiDump`
- `printEmoji` DSL function
- Optional confetti celebration on successful runs
- Configurable feature toggles for progress bar, greeting, summary, and confetti
